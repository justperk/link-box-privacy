Fly Loft privacy

Updated 18 November 2026. Fly Loft runs only on this computer and sends no data anywhere. It has no analytics, no ads and no account.

What I keep on this device

I store only what I need to split windows and undo the last split:

  • Your layout preferences (which layout to use on icon click, and any custom column widths) in the browser's local extension storage until you change them or uninstall Fly Loft.
  • A one-step Undo snapshot in the browser's session storage: window sizes and positions, tab ids and order, pinned and active state, and tab-group title, color, and collapsed. I delete that snapshot when Undo finishes, when you split again, or when you quit the browser.
  • A short last-error or last-notice string in session storage so the toolbar icon can show what happened after the popup closes.
  • This session's measured minimum window size (width and height only), so I do not open a tiny probe window on every split. That number is gone when you quit the browser.

What I do not read or keep

I do not read page URLs, titles, or contents. I do not store favicons, cookies, account identifiers, or keystrokes. I do not use sync storage.

What leaves this computer

Nothing I write. I make no network calls, I have no crash reporter, and I have no analytics. I read display sizes so windows stay on the screen you are using. Badge and tooltip text stay on this device.
