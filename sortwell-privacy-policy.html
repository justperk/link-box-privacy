Sortwell privacy

Sortwell collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never connects to the internet.

What it reads

Sortwell reads your bookmarks (names, addresses, folders, order and the date each was added) so it can show you a preview of a change. It reads them only while its page is open.

What it changes

It moves, removes or joins bookmarks and folders only after you click Apply on a preview.

What it stores

Before each change it saves a copy of your bookmarks, plus what it needs to undo the change, in the browser's storage for this add-on, on your computer. It keeps the last 3 copies and the last 5 changes. If you ask it to, it also saves a backup file to your Downloads folder. Your browser's own bookmark sync may copy the changes to your other devices, as it does for any bookmark change.

Permissions

Bookmarks: to read, sort and clean up your bookmarks. Storage: to keep the backup copies, the undo list and your settings on this computer.

Removing it

Uninstalling Sortwell deletes its stored copies and undo list. Your bookmarks stay as they are. Save a copy as HTML first (Backups tab) if you want to keep one.
