Tearsheet privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Tearsheet is a Chrome extension that copies a table, list, or highlighted text from the page you are on onto a clean sheet you can print, save as PDF or HTML, or copy. This page explains what it does with your data.

What Tearsheet collects

Nothing. Tearsheet has no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

What it reads on your computer

Tearsheet reads a page only when you ask it to: when you click its toolbar button, use its right-click menu, or press its keyboard shortcut. It then reads the part you asked for (your highlighted text, the biggest table, or the biggest list), plus the page title, address and language for the top of the sheet. If you click Next or Previous on the sheet, it reads the same page again to show another table or list. It can do that only until that tab is closed or goes to another page.

That copy stays in Chrome on your computer. The current sheet is kept in Chrome's session storage, and the last sheet is kept in Chrome's local storage so "Open last tearsheet" can reopen it. Removing the extension deletes both.

Pictures

If the part you pick has pictures, the sheet shows them from the website they came from, the same way the original page did. That website can see that the pictures were loaded, as it could when you viewed the page. Tearsheet asks the browser not to send the sheet's address to that website.

When you click Save HTML with "Put pictures inside saved HTML" turned on, Tearsheet reads those pictures again from inside the page they came from, using the copies your browser already has where it can, and stores them in the file on your computer. Pictures the website will not share stay as links to that website. Tearsheet does not upload pictures or anything else anywhere.

Saving and sharing

"Save HTML" writes a file to your computer. "Copy" puts the sheet on your clipboard. "Print or save PDF" opens Chrome's print window, where you can print or choose Save as PDF. What you do with those files is up to you.

Sharing with others

Tearsheet does not sell, share, or transfer any data, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions, or anything other than making your sheet.

Limited Use

The use of information received from this extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
