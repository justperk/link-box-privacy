Fly Loft privacy

Updated 19 August 2026. I am a local Chrome extension. I do not send data off this computer, I do not use analytics or advertising, and I do not require an account.

What I keep on this device

I store only what I need to tile windows and undo the last split:

  • Your layout preferences — which layout to apply on icon click, and any custom column ratios — in Chrome local extension storage until you change or uninstall them.
  • A one-step Undo snapshot in Chrome session storage: window sizes and positions, tab ids and order, pinned and active state, and tab-group title, color, and collapsed. I delete that snapshot when Undo finishes, when you apply a new layout, or when you quit Chrome.
  • A short last-error or last-notice string in session storage so the toolbar icon can show what happened after the popup closes.
  • This session’s measured minimum window size — width and height only — so I do not open a tiny probe window on every layout. That number is gone when you quit Chrome.

What I do not read or keep

I do not read page URLs, titles, or contents. I do not store favicons, cookies, account identifiers, or keystrokes. I do not use Chrome sync storage.

What leaves this computer

Nothing I write. I make no network calls, I have no crash reporter, and I have no analytics. I read display work-area sizes so windows stay on the monitor you are using. Badge and tooltip text stay on this device.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
