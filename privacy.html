Privacy

Effective 14 August 2026. Link Box is a local browser extension.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or sell any data. There is no account, no analytics, and no server.

On your device, Link Box reads link URLs on the page you are looking at, only when you drag a selection. Those URLs are used in memory to highlight, open tabs, or copy to the clipboard. They are not stored by the extension and are not sent to the developer.

Settings (activation key, max tabs, and the toggles on the options page) are saved with chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Google stores those settings in your Chrome profile so they follow you across devices. That is Chrome’s sync, not a Link Box server.

What we do not do

No browsing history log. No third parties. No advertising. No remote code. Selected URLs leave the page only as tabs you open or text you copy.

How to delete

Uninstall the extension. Then, if you want settings gone from Chrome Sync, clear sync data for extensions in Chrome settings, or reset the options to defaults before uninstalling.

Contact

Use the support email on the Chrome Web Store listing once published, or the developer contact on the listing.
