Link Box privacy

Updated 14 August 2026

I don't collect anything. There is no account and no server.

When you drag a box, the extension reads the link URLs on that page so it can highlight them, open them, or copy them. Those URLs stay on your machine. I never see them.

Your settings (which key you use, max tabs, the checkboxes) are saved in Chrome's own sync storage. If Chrome Sync is on, Google keeps those settings with your profile. That's Chrome, not me.

Uninstall the extension to remove it. To wipe settings, reset them first or clear extension sync data in Chrome.
