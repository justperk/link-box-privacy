Link Box privacy

Updated 18 November 2026

Link Box collects nothing. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and the developer never receives your data.

The Link Box script loads on the pages you visit so a box drag works anywhere, but it does nothing until you hold the key you chose (Shift by default) and drag. Then it reads the addresses of the links inside your box so it can highlight them, open them or copy them. Those addresses stay on your computer.

Your settings are kept in Chrome's sync storage. If Chrome Sync is on, Chrome keeps them with your Google account.

To remove Link Box, uninstall it from Chrome. Click Reset to defaults first if you also want your synced settings cleared.

Limited Use

The use of information received from this extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
